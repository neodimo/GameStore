## 2026-08-24 — launch-freeze and cover-weight performance work

- **What was done:** Omid reported the app hanging after launch, art not finishing loading, and freezing during use, and asked whether downloaded artwork was oversized. Both were real defects with separate causes. **Measured evidence:** the hang was artwork *matching*, not artwork transfer — `src/artwork.tsx` scored all 1,486 games against all 9,339 Libretro filenames inside one synchronous `useMemo` (13.9M comparisons), timed at **67,767 ms** of blocked main thread running the real matcher over the real catalog. Two fixes: filename parsing/normalization/bigram construction were hoisted into a prepared index built once per fetched file list rather than once per `(game, file)` pair (**67.8s → 4.6s** alone), and the `coverName` already present on every imported record — previously unused — now resolves **1,259/1,486 titles by map lookup in 74 ms**, leaving 227 for fuzzy scoring in 8 ms slices returned to the event loop. Full resolve is **67.8s → ~1s at an identical 1,378/1,486 match count**. Separately, artwork *was* oversized: Libretro PS1 box art is uniformly 512x512 PNG, sampled at **median 408 KB / mean 433 KB / max 741 KB (n=25)**, painted into cards a fraction of that size. Grid covers are now downscaled to 384px and re-encoded into the disposable media cache on first sight, **measured at 39.8 KB mean across real cached files (90.8% smaller)**, aspect ratio preserved; the detail view keeps the full-resolution original, and `content-visibility` lets offscreen cards release decoded bitmaps. **Inference, not measured:** the memory-growth-while-scrolling theory (decoded bitmaps accumulating) is consistent with the evidence but was not directly profiled; the ~640 MB full-scroll transfer figure is mean-size arithmetic, not an observed transfer.
